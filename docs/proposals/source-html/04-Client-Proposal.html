Why this, and why now

Right now, anyone checking out Ophir Mineral Ventures online — a buyer's compliance team, a bank processing a large transfer, a potential partner — finds nothing. A well-built site fixes that in two ways at once: it gives you something solid to point to during due diligence, and it gives you a professional space to present the company when you're trying to close a deal.

This proposal covers what the site would include, how it would be built, what it costs to run, and — since you asked directly — what your Google Workspace subscription can and can't do here.

About your Google Workspace

You mentioned not wanting a site built on the templates that come with your Google hosting — worth unpacking exactly what that subscription is and isn't, since the terms get confusing.

What you're paying for is Google Workspace: professional email on your own domain (the @ophirmineralventures.com address), plus Drive, Calendar, and related tools. It includes a very basic website builder called Google Sites — that's almost certainly the "template" look you're trying to avoid. Google Sites can't run a real content management system, can't handle a proper contact form with routing, and has no ability to build the kind of custom design shown in the attached draft.

The good news Your email doesn't need to move, change, or go through any risk at all. A domain's technical settings let email and website point to two completely different places at once — so the new site goes live on its own without touching your Workspace email in any way.

Checked whether your Workspace subscription could host the new site directly, to save you a separate hosting cost — it can't. Google doesn't offer a way to run a custom-built site like this one within a Workspace plan; that requires its own hosting, covered in the options below.

The technology, briefly

The site will be built on Umbraco, a mature, widely-used content management system that's free and open-source — no software license fee, ever, regardless of how the business grows. It runs on ASP.NET Core, Microsoft's platform for building web applications, which is what gives the finished site a genuinely custom design rather than a page-builder look.

You get a simple editing screen for text, images, and documents — similar in spirit to editing a Word document — while everything underneath stays fully custom and fully secure.
